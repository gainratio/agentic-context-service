Agentic Context Service · demo

Each AI assistant sees only what its job allows.

Companies let AI assistants read their records so they can answer questions. This service sits in between: it hands each assistant only the records it is allowed to see, and says where every fact came from and how old it is.

Offline demo

Runs only on your computer.

The story

Demo Retail is a shoe shop that sells a trail shoe called NS-100. It has two AI assistants: one helps its pricing team, one answers customers at the help desk. A second shop, Other Retailer, uses the same service. Between them, the service holds 7 records: entries from the shops' databases, such as a price rule or a product description.
